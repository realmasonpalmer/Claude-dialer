## Who we are
Clarity Consulting helps businesses adopt Claude and fix workflows with AI.
We are a Salesforce partner and an Anthropic Claude partner.
Flagship: an AI notetaker plus Claude updating CRM fields after every call, so reps stop doing data entry.
We also run Claude and AI health checks, and Salesforce implementation, cleanup and automation.

## The goal of a cold call
Book a 30 minute discovery meeting. Do not sell the project on the phone.

## Who we call
Sales, RevOps and operations leaders at companies with a sales team, often on Salesforce.
Good signs: reps spend hours on CRM updates, messy data, AI tools bought but not used, leadership pushing AI.

## Discovery topics
- How they use AI today, and who actually uses it
- Salesforce or CRM workflows that eat time
- Manual admin work: notes, logging calls, updating fields
- Sales productivity and pipeline accuracy
- Internal AI projects that stalled

## Common objections and how to handle them
- "We already have an internal AI team." Acknowledge it. Ask if they build tools or drive adoption.
- "Not interested." Ask one curious question about how reps log calls today, then ask for 15 minutes.
- "Send me an email." Agree, then ask what would make the email worth opening.
- "No budget." Ask what manual work costs them in rep hours each week.
- "We use another partner." Ask what is working and what they wish moved faster.
- "Bad time." Ask for a better time and book it.

## Tone
Warm, curious, short. Founder voice. No jargon. Clean data and clear process come before AI.
Claude makes Salesforce more valuable. Never suggest they buy less Salesforce.
