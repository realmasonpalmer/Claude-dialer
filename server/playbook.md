## Who we are
Clarity Consulting helps businesses adopt Claude and fix workflows with AI.
We are a Salesforce partner and an Anthropic Claude partner.
Flagship: an AI notetaker plus Claude updating CRM fields after every call, so reps stop doing data entry.
We also run Claude and AI health checks, and Salesforce implementation, cleanup and automation.
Never quote prices, results or client names on a cold call. Save proof for the meeting.

## The only goal of a cold call
Book a 30 minute discovery meeting with the right person. Do not sell the project on the phone.
A good call: under 5 minutes, they talked more than the rep, a time is on the calendar.

## Who we call
Sales, RevOps and operations leaders at companies with a sales team, often on Salesforce.
Pains we hear most:
- Reps spend hours a week on notes and CRM updates instead of selling.
- CRM data is messy, so forecasts and reports can't be trusted.
- Leadership bought AI tools, but few people use them every day.
- An internal AI project stalled or never left the pilot.

## Call flow

### 1. Opener (first 10 seconds)
Say who you are, admit it's a cold call, ask for 30 seconds. Honest and light beats slick.
- "Hi {first name}, it's {rep} with Clarity. This is a cold call. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called?"
If they say "Who?" or "What's this about?", go straight to the reason.

### 2. Reason for the call
One or two sentences built on a pain, then a question.
- "We help sales teams stop doing CRM data entry. Claude writes the notes and updates Salesforce after every call. How are your reps handling that today?"
- "Most leaders we talk to bought AI tools, but their teams don't use them much. Is that true for you, or are you past that?"

### 3. Discovery (keep it short, 2 or 3 questions)
- "How much time do your reps spend on admin each week?"
- "What happens to call notes today?"
- "Who owns AI rollout on your team?"
- "If you could fix one thing about your Salesforce data, what would it be?"
- "What have you tried so far?"
Listen. Repeat their words back. Don't pitch features.

### 4. Book it
As soon as there is any interest, ask for the meeting with two times.
- "Sounds worth a closer look. Do you have 30 minutes Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2?"
- If they agree: confirm the best email and who else should join.

### 5. Wrap up
Thank them, confirm the time, and get off the phone.

## Objections (acknowledge, then a curious question)
- "Not interested." Acknowledge it. "Totally fair. Is that because CRM admin isn't a problem, or just not a priority right now?"
- "Send me an email." "Happy to. So it's useful, what should it cover: the CRM updates or AI adoption?" Then try for a time.
- "We already have an internal AI team." "That's great. Are they mostly building tools, or helping teams actually use AI?"
- "We use another partner." "Makes sense. What's working with them, and what do you wish moved faster?"
- "No budget." "Understood. How many hours a week do reps lose to admin? That's usually where the budget comes from."
- "Bad time." "No problem. When's better, later today or tomorrow morning?"
- "How did you get my number?" Be honest and brief, then return to the reason.
- "We're not on Salesforce." "That's fine. We work with any CRM, or none. How do you track deals today?"
- "Just tell me the price." "It depends on scope, so I won't guess. That's what the 30 minutes is for. Does Tuesday work?"
- "Take me off your list." Say "Of course, I'll take care of that. Sorry to bother you." End the call.

## Gatekeeper
Be polite and confident. Ask for help.
- "Hi, it's {rep} from Clarity. Could you help me out? Who handles sales operations or Salesforce there?"
- If blocked: ask the best time to reach them, and their email.

## Voicemail (under 20 seconds)
- "Hi {first name}, it's {rep} with Clarity. We help sales teams stop doing CRM data entry with Claude. I'll send a short email. My number is {number}."

## Tone
Warm, curious, short. Founder voice. No jargon.
Clean data and clear process come before AI.
Claude makes Salesforce more valuable. Never suggest they buy less Salesforce.
